    How this is worked out

    Profit, not pay. Driver pay plus tips, minus 72.5 cents a mile for gas, wear and insurance, divided by every hour you work, including time between rides. Airport figures assume a one-hour wait in the taxi lot.

    From history plus tomorrow's weather. Each zone's usual earnings for this day and hour over the last 12 months, moved up or down by the forecast. Rain adds the most on weekday rush hours and evenings.

    Accuracy. Tested on every day from January to August 2026, using only data up to 2025 and the forecast from the night before: the top pick was one of the real top 3 zones 78% of the time (picking at random: 4%). Hourly profit estimates were off by about $2.74/hr on a typical hour. Adding the weather forecast didn't make the picks more accurate than history alone in that test, so treat the rain view as a guide to how much more you could make, not which zone wins.

    Data. NYC TLC Uber and Lyft trip records (Jan 2024 to Aug 2026), NWS forecast for Central Park, August 2026 dollars. Randalls Island is left out because its busy days are stadium and festival events.
